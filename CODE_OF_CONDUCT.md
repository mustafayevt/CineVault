# Code of conduct

CineVault follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in its spaces, is expected to follow it.

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of different views and experience, accept constructive criticism gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub: [@mustafayevt](https://github.com/mustafayevt). For problems that should not be discussed publicly, you can also open a private security advisory (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)) and mark it as a conduct report. All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code of conduct, and may block contributors for behaviour they deem inappropriate, as described in the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
