How it works

Scan, Scrape, Rename.

One verb per job. Every title moves through three stages, and you always see where each one stands.

  1. 1

    Scan

    New

    Automatic. Add your movie and TV folders and CineVault finds every title, keeps its extra files with it and watches for changes. NFO files already on disk are read first, so titles that have them arrive already Scraped.

  2. 2

    Scrape

    Scraped

    When you ask. Each title gets a match with a confidence score, and Accept all confident takes the sure ones in one click. Every Scrape writes the complete NFO and all the artwork next to the file.

  3. 3

    Rename

    Renamed

    From a template. A word-level preview shows exactly what changes, conflicts are settled on their own row, and nothing moves until you say so. History can put it all back.

Library

Movies, shows and collections, at a glance

A poster grid or a table where every column sorts. Filter by stage, drive, quality or problems. TV shows open on their seasons, with missing and unaired episodes marked, and a collection appears by itself once you own two films of the same set.

  • Show, season and episode pages with season artwork
  • Automatic collections, with the films you are missing
  • Problems filter for unmatched titles and missing files

Movie page

The whole film on one page

Plot, cast and crew, ratings side by side and the real video and audio formats read from the file. Every rating links to the movie's page on its site.

  • TMDB with every Scrape; IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic, Letterboxd, Trakt and more with your own free OMDb or MDBList key
  • Resolution, HDR and Dolby Vision, audio formats and subtitles from the file
  • Pick any poster, fanart, logo, clear art, banner, disc art or thumb in the artwork picker

Match picker

Know the match before you take it

Every candidate comes with a confidence score and the reason for it. Pick another one and CineVault shows what will change first: the title, the director, the runtime against your file, and the files it rewrites.

  • Accept all confident takes every sure match at once
  • Search by title and year, or paste a link or an ID
  • Complete Kodi, Jellyfin and Emby NFO and all artwork on every Scrape

Rename

Tidy names, and nothing moves until you say so

Pick a naming style or write your own template. The preview strikes out the words that go and marks the ones that come, and a clash with another title is settled right on its row.

beforeDune.Part.Two.2024.2160p.WEB-DL.DV.HDR10.TrueHD.Atmos.7.1-GRP
afterDune - Part Two (2024)

Template {Title} ({Year})

Drives

See where the space went

Free space on every drive, the titles on each one biggest first, and a warning when a drive runs low. Move titles to another drive and CineVault copies, verifies the copy, and only then sends the originals to the Recycle Bin.

  • Copy, verify, then the originals to the Recycle Bin
  • Quality and size of every title, per drive
  • Offline drives keep their titles until they come back

Torrent search

Find a better copy

Search a movie or a show from inside the app. Filter by resolution and see at once whether each release is the Same as, Better or Worse than the copy you own. CineVault only hands the release you pick to your own torrent app.

  • Results with quality, size, seeders, source and age
  • Search a whole show, one season or one episode

A short notice appears on your first visit: You are responsible for what you download and share.

History

Undo for everything that touches your files

Renames, moves, deletes, artwork and match changes are all kept in History, each with its own Undo. Delete sends a title to the Recycle Bin; erasing it for good takes Shift+Delete and a typed confirmation.

  • Undo a whole batch or a single change
  • Replaced artwork is kept, so Undo puts it back

And also

The little things

  • Your media centre reads it

    NFO files and artwork in the Kodi, Jellyfin and Emby formats, kept up to date every time something changes.

  • Light and dark

    Follows your system theme, with zoom from 80% to 150% for any screen.

  • Quick with the keyboard

    Arrow keys, Enter, Delete, Esc and Ctrl+F for search cover the everyday work.

  • Private by design

    No account and no telemetry. Your own provider keys stay in the system's credential store.

  • Nothing lost

    Deletes go to the Recycle Bin, leftovers to a trash folder, and every rename can be undone.

  • Fast on big libraries

    A Rust core and a poster grid that stays smooth with thousands of titles.
